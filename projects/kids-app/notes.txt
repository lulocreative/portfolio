PROJECT NAME: Yummo Adventures – Cenovis Kids Storybook App
CLIENT / COMPANY: Cenovis (agency: Pusher, Sydney)
YEAR: 2012
MARKETS: Australia
PLATFORM: Tablet app, iOS and Android (check)

MY ROLE: Digital Art Director (concept, design and animation)

STARTING POINT: a Word document with the kids' story and dialogue. Nothing else.
Everything visual, and the game concepts, came from me.

LED:
- The creative idea: turning the story into an app world (Yummo)
- The concept for all four games (Super Charge, Wipe Out, Get Netted, Bed Head)

MADE MYSELF:
- Character design: Sammy and Sally, fruit characters, germs, fish, sleep enemies
- Illustration of all characters and worlds
- Storyboards for the stories
- Animation of the stories and characters

THE CHALLENGE (from Behance):
"A huge 34% of Aussie kids do not eat the minimum recommended daily number of serves
of fruit and vegetables. Cenovis wanted to reach parents and their kids, teaching them
the importance of good nutrition and vitamins in a fun and positive way."

THE IDEA (from Behance, the text is cut off in the original image):
"To show kids the benefits of eating well Pusher created Yummo Adventures, a series of
games for the tablet (both iOS and an[droid?]). The games take kids on an adventure where
they learn about 'Good Stuff' like the vitamins found in fruit and vegetables..."

STORYBOOK: animated stories with Sammy and Sally ("Sammy gets sleepy", "Sally gets sniffly"...)

GAMES (from Behance):
- SUPER CHARGE: Sammy and Sally run through Yummo collecting fruit and vegetables to keep
  their energy up while avoiding candy rocks. Speed and difficulty increase over time.
- WIPE OUT: squash nasty germs before they make Sammy and Sally sneeze. 3 lives,
  multivitamin power-ups give temporary protection.
- GET NETTED: catch the fish jumping out of the lake with a net to earn fish oil points.
  Gets faster and more complex as you improve.
- BED HEAD: Sammy and Sally bounce on floating beds in a dream, reaching for flying apples
  while avoiding "sleep-depriving" things like TVs and video games. Timing and rhythm game.

CHARACTERS: Sammy and Sally, fruit characters, germ monsters, candy rocks, fish, sleep enemies
(TV, game controller, light bulb, moon).
